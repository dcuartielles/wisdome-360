# Wisdome 360 logo

The wordmark is "wisdome 360" with the **o drawn as a dome master**: the circle of the file, with the sweet spot (the amber dot) sitting below centre, towards the front edge of the master. It is drawn at about 0.27 of the radius. Since the venue's 2 Nov 2026 correction (160° masters, sweet spot about 20° above eye level), the real sweet spot sits lower, at about 0.56 of the radius: 44° of the 80° from the centre to the edge. The mark keeps the idea; it isn't a measurement.

Colours: ink `#1d3557`, sweet spot `#D0862A`, white on dark.

**Rules**
- Keep the dot below centre. The offset is the point of the mark.
- Leave clear space around the wordmark at least the height of the "o".
- Don't recolour the dot, rotate the mark or set the wordmark in another font.
- It is our own mark: it doesn't borrow from Wisdome's or Malmö Museum's branding, and it shouldn't be used to look like theirs.
- **Next to the Malmö University logo:** use the official MaU logo files and follow MaU's brand guidelines for co-branding (placement, size, clear space). Don't redraw or recolour the MaU logo.
