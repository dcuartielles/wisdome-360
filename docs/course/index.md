# MaU course 2026: Wisdome week

For students on the one-week module in **EDT 26** (master) and **TEI 26** (bachelor), Malmö University, **9–13 November 2026**. Your group makes a piece for Wisdome Malmö and shows it there on Friday 13 November.

This section has what is specific to the course: the week, the two tracks, deadlines and how to hand in. The rest of the site is general and applies to you too.

## Do these three things first

1. **Read [the week](week.md)** so you know what has to exist by the end of each day.
2. **Choose your track with your group** on Monday (below). It decides what you hand in.
3. **Run the [setup wizard](../setup/wizard.html)** and get one example playing in the Dome Simulator on your own laptop.

## Two tracks

| | **Track A — a film** | **Track B — live** |
|---|---|---|
| In one line | You deliver a film. We play it. | You bring a laptop. It runs live. |
| What you hand in | A dome-master video and separate audio files | A working system on your laptop **and** a fallback recording |
| How it reaches the dome | Joined with every other Track A film into one reel | You plug in your laptop: **NDI video + Dante audio** (two Ethernet cables, 5.1), **or HDMI** (one cable, stereo only) |
| Risk on Friday | Almost none | Real: machines fail. Hence the fallback |
| Deadline | Files by **Friday 08:00** | Fallback recording by **Friday 08:00**, laptop at the dry run |
| How to make it | [Making a film](../make/film.md) | [Making a live piece](../make/live.md) |

Both tracks are welcome. Track B has no cap, but every Track B group must hand in a fallback recording that meets the [delivery spec](../reference/delivery-spec.md). If your laptop fails, the recording plays and your presentation goes on.
