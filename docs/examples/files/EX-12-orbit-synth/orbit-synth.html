Orbit synth

Three spheres of mesh orbit in a void: a keyboard, drums and a guitar. Readers circle and hum; lines are notes. Every sound comes from one AudioWorklet synthesiser.

Play

Click a cell: a line. Click a line: another reader. Shift-click a line: remove it. Shift + drag: turn a sphere. Double-click a sphere from outside: fly into it.

Stress test

Raise the lines until real-time drops below about 98 % or the picture stutters: that is this computer's limit.
