# Making work for the Wisdome dome

In one week your group makes a piece for **Wisdome Malmö**, the tilted 11.6 m dome at Malmö Museum, and shows it there on **Friday 13 November**. This site has everything technical: how the dome works, how to set up a rehearsal rig on your own laptop, worked examples you can copy, the problems groups usually hit, and the exact file you hand in.

## Do these three things first

1. **Read [the week](week.md)** so you know what has to exist by the end of each day.
2. **Choose your track with your group** on Monday (below). It decides what you hand in.
3. **Run the [setup wizard](setup/wizard.html)** and get one example playing in the Dome Simulator on your own laptop. Everything after that is your own piece.

## Two tracks

| | **Track A — a film** | **Track B — live** |
|---|---|---|
| In one line | You deliver a film. We play it. | You bring a machine. It runs live. |
| What you hand in | A dome-master video and separate audio files | A working system on your laptop **and** a fallback recording |
| How it reaches the dome | Joined with every other Track A film into one reel | You plug your laptop in: video by NDI or HDMI, audio by Dante |
| Risk on Friday | Almost none | Real — machines fail. Hence the fallback |
| Deadline | Files by **Friday 08:00** | Fallback recording by **Friday 08:00**, machine at the dry run |

Both tracks are welcome. Track B has no cap, but every Track B group must bring a fallback recording that meets the [delivery spec](delivery.md). If your machine fails, the recording plays and your presentation goes on.

## The one idea behind everything here

**Compose by angle, not by pixel, and measure from the seats.** The dome is tilted 26°, raised at the back, so the centre of a dome master is not overhead: it is 64° up, in front of the audience. Put your subject in the patch in front, 15°–60° above eye level and within 60° either side, aimed at the **sweet spot, straight ahead and 40° up**. [Where to put things](reference/comfortable-zone.md) explains why.
