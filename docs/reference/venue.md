# Wisdome Malmö

The dome at Malmö Museum, opened in April 2023 and one of five Wisdome domes in Sweden. Malmö University is a partner of Wisdome Malmö.

## Confirmed by the venue

| | |
|---|---|
| Dome | Astro-Tec, about 11.6 m, **tilted 26°**, raised at the back |
| Seats | 58 |
| Projection | 5 projectors over a **160°** screen. The player spreads the **whole** circle of a dome master over it: nothing is cropped |
| Render at | **160°** fisheye, not 180° |
| Centre of the image | 64° above eye level, straight ahead |
| Edge of the screen | 16° below eye level at the front, 36° above eye level behind the audience |
| Sweet spot | The venue's "40" = **about 20° above eye level**, straight ahead (see [Where to put things](comfortable-zone.md)) |
| Live picture | **HDMI** at 3840 × 2160, with the dome master centred as a 2160² square |
| Live sound | **Dante**, 6 channels, 5.1 (L, R, C, LFE, Ls, Rs). Stereo over HDMI as a fallback |
| Resolution | 2048 × 2048 is a good start; 6144 × 6144 is the maximum |
| Slideshows | 16:9, about 2K high, content in the centre (or rendered through a 160° fisheye, see EX-08) |

## What this means for you

- **Render your fisheye at 160°.** A 180° master plays, but everything is squeezed towards the centre by 8/9.
- **Start at 2K.** 6K is the ceiling, not the target, and it multiplies your render time by nine.
- **A live piece sends its picture over HDMI and its sound over Dante.** See [Connecting a live piece](connecting.md).
- **Aim at the sweet spot, about 20° up**, not higher: the screen starts 16° below eye level at the front.
