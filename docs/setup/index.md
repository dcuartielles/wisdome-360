# Set up your rehearsal rig

You will see your piece in the real dome only on Friday morning. Until then you rehearse on your own laptop, with the **Dome Simulator**: a browser page that shows your dome master from a seat inside a virtual Wisdome, with the comfortable zone and sweet spot drawn over it.

[Start the setup wizard](wizard.html){ .primary }
[Open the Dome Simulator](../tools/dome-simulator.html)
</div>

The **wizard** asks four questions (your track, your computer, your tool, your hardware) and gives you a checklist for your setup only. It remembers your answers and ticks on this computer, so you can come back to it.

## What you need

- A laptop with Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Safari is untested.
- For a live piece (Track B): the tool you will use, and later two Ethernet ports (USB adapters are fine).
- Optional: a USB HDMI capture card, if you want to test a real second display.

## The three ways in

| | What it is | Who needs it |
|---|---|---|
| **Dome Simulator** | Open a file, or share a window, and see it in the dome | Everyone |
| **Virtual Dome monitor** | Gives your laptop a second, invisible 2048 × 2048 screen to go fullscreen on | Track B, when sharing a window isn't clean enough |
| **NDI bridge** | Receives NDI, the signal the venue takes, and feeds it to the simulator | Track B, to test the real connection |

The virtual monitor and the NDI bridge are downloads; the wizard links to the right one for your computer.
