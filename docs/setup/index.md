# Set up your rehearsal rig

Time in the real dome is short, so most of your rehearsing happens on your own laptop, with the **Dome Simulator**: a browser page that shows your dome master from a seat inside a virtual Wisdome, with the comfortable zone and sweet spot drawn over it.

<div class="w360-go" markdown>
[Start the setup wizard](wizard.html){ .primary }
[Open the Dome Simulator](../tools/dome-simulator.html)
</div>

The **wizard** asks four questions (your track, your computer, your tool, your hardware) and gives you a checklist for your setup only. It remembers your answers and ticks on this computer, so you can come back to it.

## What you need

- A laptop with Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Safari is untested.
- For a live piece: the tool you will use. Later, for the venue, either two Ethernet ports for NDI + Dante (USB adapters are fine) or an HDMI output. See [Connecting a live piece](../reference/connecting.md).
- Optional: a USB HDMI capture card, if you want to test a real second display.

## The three ways in

| | What it is | Who needs it |
|---|---|---|
| **Dome Simulator** | Open a file, or share a window, and see it in the dome | Everyone |
| **Virtual Dome monitor** | Gives your laptop a second, invisible 2048 × 2048 screen to go fullscreen on | Live pieces, when sharing a window isn't clean enough |
| **NDI bridge** | Receives NDI, the venue's network video input, and feeds it to the simulator | Live pieces using NDI + Dante |
| **Capture card** | A USB HDMI capture card; the simulator shows what your laptop sends over HDMI | Live pieces using HDMI |

The virtual monitor and the NDI bridge are downloads; the wizard links to the right one for your computer.
