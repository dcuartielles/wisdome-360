# Pictures and films not included in this copy

These live slides were shown in Wisdome Malmö on 9 November 2026 with pictures and films that belong to
other people. They are **not in this repository**: we don't have the right to redistribute them. Every
slide still runs without them and shows a dashed card saying which file it is waiting for.

To show them yourself, download each one from its source, check that its licence allows your use,
and save it in the slide's `media/` folder under the name given here. Then reload the picture page.
